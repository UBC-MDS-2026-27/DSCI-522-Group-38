# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making this data science project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive space for everyone. We value diversity and welcome contributions regardless of age, disability, gender identity or expression, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, socioeconomic background, level of experience, or any other personal characteristic.

Everyone involved in this project should be able to participate without fear of harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disrespect. We recognize that people have different perspectives, skills, and levels of experience, and we will treat one another with patience and consideration.

## Expected Behaviour

All team members and project contributors are expected to:

* Treat others with respect, kindness, and professionalism.
* Listen to different perspectives and give feedback in a constructive and considerate way.
* Welcome questions and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
* Communicate clearly, honestly, and respectfully in meetings, discussions, code reviews, and online spaces.
* Give credit for other people's ideas, work, and contributions.
* Respect agreed team processes, deadlines, and responsibilities, and communicate early when difficulties arise.
* Keep project discussions focused on the work while making room for everyone to participate.

## Unexpected Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Offensive comments, jokes, or content related to personal characteristics such as race, gender, disability, religion, or sexual orientation.
* Unwanted sexual attention, advances, or comments.
* Threatening, abusive, or deliberately humiliating language or behaviour.
* Deliberately disrupting discussions or repeatedly preventing others from contributing.
* Sharing someone's private information, messages, or work without their permission.
* Taking credit for another person's work or knowingly misrepresenting contributions.
* Retaliating against anyone who raises a concern or participates in an investigation.

The response will be proportionate to the situation. Serious incidents may result in immediate escalation without a prior warning.

## Reporting and Response Process

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, send an email to the team's slack channel: <aaaaw7kvifpvcyqmicjdgegsmu@ubc-mds.slack.com>. A team member may also contact the course instructor if they are uncomfortable reporting the issue to the team.

When making a report, include as much relevant information as you feel comfortable sharing, such as what happened, when and where it happened, and who was involved. You do not need to provide evidence before raising a concern.

The team will:
- Acknowledge the report as soon as reasonably possible.
- Handle the report respectfully and share details only with people who need them to respond.
- Listen to the people involved and consider the available information fairly.
- Take appropriate action based on the nature and seriousness of the incident.
- Avoid retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

We will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of everyone involved. However, complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed if further action is required to address the concern or meet course or university obligations.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces and activities, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, code reviews, team meetings, messages, and project presentations.
